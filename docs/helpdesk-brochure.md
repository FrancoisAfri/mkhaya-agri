# Mkhaya HelpDesk
### Enterprise IT Service Management — built for the modern service desk

A single, integrated platform to **log, track, resolve and report** on every IT incident, problem, change and request — across email, web, live chat and mobile. Designed around ITIL best practice, POPIA-aware, and fully brandable to your organisation.

---

## Why Mkhaya HelpDesk

- **One platform, the full ITIL lifecycle** — Incidents, Problems, Changes, Knowledge and Assets in one place, not five disconnected tools.
- **Multi-channel by design** — users reach you however they prefer: email, self-service portal, live chat, or logged on their behalf by an agent.
- **Proactive, not reactive SLAs** — the system warns you *before* a breach, not after.
- **No-code administration** — SLAs, business hours, categories, workflows and branding are all configured by admins, never by developers.
- **Built-in business intelligence** — dashboards, graphs and exportable reports out of the box.
- **Secure & compliant** — role-based access, full audit trail, encryption, and POPIA-aligned data handling.

---

## 1. Multi-Channel Ticket Logging

Capture every request, no matter where it comes from.

- **Email-to-ticket** — inbound mail automatically creates a ticket, with duplicate detection so nothing is logged twice.
- **Self-service portal** — end-users log and track their own tickets, on-premise or remotely.
- **Agent-logged tickets** — Help Desk Administrators and ICT staff capture walk-up and phone requests manually.
- **Live chat** — real-time conversations that convert directly into tickets.
- **Mobile app** — ICT staff log, acknowledge and manage tickets on the move.

---

## 2. Incident Management

A complete, auditable incident lifecycle.

- Incidents logged via **portal, email, live chat, or manually**.
- **Instant reference number** issued the moment a ticket is created.
- **Re-classification, prioritisation and categorisation** by permitted ICT staff.
- **Multi-level categories** (e.g. *Hardware → Server → Power Supply*).
- **Priority driven by impact and urgency**.
- **Reassignment between agents** with a full audit trail (who reassigned, to whom, and when).
- **Email replies from within the system** — agent responses go out as email; user replies thread back onto the same ticket automatically.
- **Ticket merging** — consolidate duplicate or related tickets.
- **Automated satisfaction survey (CSAT)** sent to the end-user when an incident is resolved.
- **Captured on every ticket:** subject; description; logger name & surname; date logged; department; contact details (email & phone); assigned agent; and status (**Open · Pending/In Progress · Resolved · Closed**).

---

## 3. SLA & Ticket Management

Stay ahead of every deadline.

- **Mean Time to Acknowledge / Respond** — measured from reference-number generation to first response.
- **Mean Time to Resolve** and **Mean Time to Close** — automatically calculated.
- **Definable SLA targets** — set and amend SLA and resolution times per priority, **no coding required**.
- **Business-hours-aware** — SLA clocks respect your defined working hours and holidays.
- **Pre-breach warnings** — agents and supervisors are notified *before* an SLA is violated.
- **Automatic escalation** — breached SLAs escalate to supervisors with notifications.
- **SLA breach filtering** — instantly find and search every ticket at risk or in violation.
- **Reopen control** — resolved tickets can be reopened by a supervisor or Help Desk Administrator.

---

## 4. Problem Management

Turn recurring incidents into permanent fixes.

- **Create and track problem records**.
- **Link multiple incidents to a single problem** for root-cause analysis.
- **Auto-close linked incidents** when the parent problem is resolved.

---

## 5. Change Management

Govern every change from request to results.

- **End-to-end change process** — from inception through to approval.
- **Structured change record:** change request ID; description; date of implementation; risk assessment; backout plan; change results.
- **Stakeholder comments captured separately:** approver, implementer, evaluator and tester.
- **Role-based workflow** across requestor → evaluator → approver → tester.
- **Works for people without system access** — participants can respond by email, and their input auto-updates the change record.

---

## 6. Asset Management Integration

Connect tickets to the hardware they're about.

- **Associate any ticket** (incident or problem) **with an ICT asset**.
- **Asset-based reporting** — generate and filter reports by asset type.
- **Standardised asset categories** — laptops, servers, switches, routers and more.
