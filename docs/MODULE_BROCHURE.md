### Employee Surveys
**Listen, measure and act on employee sentiment — at scale.**
A complete survey and feedback engine spanning quick pulse checks to full 360-degree reviews, with smart logic, automation and action planning built in.

- **Survey types out of the box:** pulse checks, engagement surveys, eNPS (employee Net Promoter Score), 360-degree multi-rater feedback, and fully custom surveys
- **Powerful question builder — 11 question types:** rating scales, Likert, multiple choice, yes/no, NPS, matrix, ranking, sliders, date pickers, open text and file uploads
- **Smart conditional logic:** branching/skip logic shows or hides questions based on previous answers — shorter, more relevant surveys and higher completion rates
- **Question controls:** randomisation, progress bar, weighted scoring, drag-and-drop reordering, and save-and-resume
- **Anonymity, secured:** anonymous mode with encrypted tracking that still prevents duplicate submissions
- **360-degree feedback:** invite peers, managers, direct reports, stakeholders and external raters; track invitations/completions and compare results across rater groups
- **Distribution & automation:** target all employees, by department or custom filters; **recurring surveys** (daily → quarterly) that auto-generate and dispatch; automated reminder emails to non-responders
- **Reach beyond the org:** token-based **public survey links** for customers, partners and external stakeholders — multi-language support included
- **Analytics & action:** real-time completion tracking, queued report exports, scheduled report distribution, and **action plans** that assign owners, due dates and status to survey insights
- Full activity audit logging on every survey, question and response

### Recognition & Rewards
**Build a culture of appreciation — social, gamified and automatic.**

- **Social recognition wall:** public, chronological feed of recognitions across the company — every win is seen
- **Peer-to-peer & top-down:** anyone can recognise anyone, with a title, message and category
- **Points economy:** customisable default points per category (with per-post overrides) and running employee point balances
- **Leaderboards:** monthly rankings by points earned, recognition count and top categories — healthy, transparent competition
- **Social engagement:** emoji reactions and threaded comments turn recognition into viral culture moments
- **Service milestones:** automatic anniversary tracking (1, 5, 10, 25 years…) with recognition tie-ins so no milestone is missed
- **Personal profiles:** per-employee given/received counts and point balances
- **Category administration:** custom categories with icons, colours and default points; enable/disable without deleting
- Full activity audit logging and compliance-grade retention

### Whistleblower & Ethics
**Enterprise-grade ethics reporting — anonymous, AI-assisted and regulator-ready.**
A secure speak-up platform covering the full journey from confidential intake to investigation, retaliation protection and board-level compliance reporting.

- **Anonymous-first intake:** public reporting portal with **no login required**; open to employees and external parties (suppliers, partners, customers)
- **Privacy by design:** reporter identity (name, email, phone, employee ID) stored with AES encryption; **follow-up tokens** let anonymous reporters check status and keep communicating; secure sessions hardened with IP/user-agent hashing
- **Structured intake:** auto-generated case references (WB-YYYY-#####), allegation categories (fraud, discrimination, safety, retaliation, ethics, compliance…), severity grading (A–F), priority, confidentiality levels and accused role/department tracking
- **AI case intelligence:** automated 0–100 risk scoring, AI tags, executive summaries with key findings & recommended actions, **retaliation-risk flagging**, suggested investigator skills, and AI **case-linking** that surfaces related historical cases
- **Full investigation workflow:** lead + co-investigator assignment, investigation plans & methodology, witness management, investigation notes, target dates with overdue/escalation tracking, and a status lifecycle from assigned → completed
- **Findings & outcomes:** substantiated/unsubstantiated classifications, evidence summaries, remediation plans, and flags for disciplinary action, criminal referral or regulatory notification — with a formal approval workflow
- **AI evidence & timeline tools:** analyse uploaded evidence for relevance and contradictions, generate case timelines, and verify events
- **Retaliation protection (a standout):** detect retaliation risk, define protection periods, assign protective measures (transfer, leave, monitoring, legal/HR support), and monitor employment treatment, disciplinary actions and manager behaviour post-report — with automatic escalation alerts
- **Policy enforcement & SLAs:** log policy violations and exceptions, per-category escalation/investigation SLAs, required-approval counts, board-notification triggers and an AI classification-rules engine
- **Secure two-way messaging:** encrypted, token-gated messaging between investigators and anonymous reporters
- **Compliance & board reporting:** executive dashboard, ethics reports, fraud-trend analytics, compliance summaries, regulator-ready exports and a complete immutable activity log

### Digital Signature (E-Sign)
**Send, sign and prove documents — legally and securely.**
A built-in e-signature engine for offers, contracts, NDAs and policy acknowledgements, with identity verification and a court-ready audit trail.

- **Envelope management:** upload documents, set title, message and expiry; classify by type (offer letter, employment contract, NDA, policy acknowledgement…); full lifecycle of draft → sent → in progress → completed, with void and auto-expiry (14-day default)
- **Multi-signer workflows:** sequential signing order (e.g. manager → executive → employee) with roles for signer, approver and witness; add/edit signers before sending; auto-dispatched invitation emails
- **Identity verification (OTP):** one-time-password challenge via email or SMS before signing — hashed codes, expiry, attempt limits and rate-limiting — meeting ESIGN/eIDAS expectations
- **Frictionless signing portal:** token-gated, **no account required** — ideal for executives, contractors and external parties; document review, explicit e-signature consent, sign or decline-with-reason, IP/user-agent captured
- **Real-time status tracking:** pending → notified → viewed → OTP sent → verified → signed (or declined), visible to HR at a glance
- **Tamper-evident documents:** SHA-256 hashing of originals, background generation of the final signed PDF, and downloadable completion certificate
- **Court-ready audit trail:** every event (created, sent, viewed, OTP, signed, declined, voided, expired) logged immutably with actor, IP, user-agent and timestamp
- **Integrated:** link envelopes directly to HR, contract and procurement records
