### Win more tenders — from discovery to signed submission, in one AI-powered workspace

*Part of the Mkhaya Hub · Operations & Support suite*

---

## Why Tender Pursuit?

Bidding is won or lost long before submission day. Scattered spreadsheets, last-minute
scrambles, missed briefings, and "should we even bid?" guesswork cost you winnable work.

**Tender Pursuit** brings the entire bid lifecycle into one place — and puts an AI bid
manager alongside your team at every step: finding opportunities that fit you, telling you
whether you qualify, drafting the proposal, pricing it competitively, compiling a branded,
signed submission pack, and making sure nothing is late.

> From "we found a tender" to "submitted and ready to win" — without leaving the system.

## What you get

### 1. AI Tender Discovery — opportunities come to you
- Automatically pulls tender opportunities from external feeds (OCDS / eTenders).
- **Matches every opportunity against your company expertise** — industries, regions,
  certifications and track record — so you only see what fits.
- AI **match-score** and pursue / review / skip recommendation, with a one-line reason.
- One click to convert a promising opportunity into a live tender.

### 2. AI Tender Qualification — "do we even qualify?"
- Give the tender's document URL and the AI **downloads it, reads it, and checks you against
  the requirements** — CIDB grading, B-BBEE, experience, certifications, turnover, references.
- Clear verdict: **Qualifies / Partially / Does not**, with a fit score.
- Lists exactly **what you meet**, your **gaps**, and **risks to verify** — before you spend
  a cent chasing an unwinnable bid.

### 3. Bid / No-Bid decision — made with evidence
- Structured decision matrix (qualification, experience, capacity, profitability, strategy…).
- **AI recommendation** with win probability, risk score and profit potential.
- Every decision recorded — defensible governance, not gut feel.

### 4. Company Expertise & Branding profile
- Capture your areas of expertise, industries, regions served, certifications, CIDB grading,
  years of experience and **past-project track record** — once.
- This profile powers the AI discovery matching **and** qualification checks.
- Upload your logo or full letterhead, colours and company particulars — used to **design
  every generated document**.

### 5. Proposal Plan & Compliance
- AI-generated task plan and responsibility matrix to keep the bid on track.
- AI-generated **compliance checklist** so no mandatory requirement slips through.
- Live progress tracking across the whole workspace.

### 6. AI Proposal Writer & Pricing
- Draft executive summaries, methodologies and proposal sections in your tone.
- Full cost sheet / **Bill of Quantities** with markup, discount and VAT.
- **AI price guide** suggests a competitive winning range based on your cost base.

### 7. Document Studio — compile the whole tender online
- Generate **branded PDFs** on your letterhead: cover letter, executive summary, technical
  proposal, methodology, company profile and quotation.
- **Sign online** — directors apply their signature on screen, embedded into the PDF.
- **Submission Pack:** a checklist of every document; include/exclude with a tick.
  - **Online submission?** Review the pack and submit, capturing the portal reference.
  - **Physical submission?** Download the complete pack as a ZIP to print and hand in.

### 8. Never miss a date
- Tender calendar with briefings, questions-closing, submission, evaluation and award dates.
- **Briefing details** captured: compulsory or optional, physical (with venue) or online
  (with meeting link).
- Automatic reminders at 30 / 14 / 7 / 3 / 1 / 0 days to close, plus briefing reminders.
- **At-risk escalation:** if a tender is close to closing but far from ready, executives and
  managers are alerted automatically — with the exact outstanding items.

### 9. Collaboration, intelligence & insight
- Team comments and @mentions on every tender.
- **AI War Room** — a live submission-readiness score across the whole bid.
- **Competitor intelligence** and **performance analytics** (win rate, pipeline value, trends).
- **AI Executive Copilot** — ask questions about your pipeline in plain language.
- **Knowledge Library** — reusable company profile, certifications, methodologies and past
  projects that feed the AI.

- **Full audit trail** — decisions, signatures and changes are recorded.
