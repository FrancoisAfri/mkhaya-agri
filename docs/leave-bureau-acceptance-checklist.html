Before a live demonstration

Your worked example takes leave 10–12 September and expects 3 days. In 2026 the 12th is a Saturday, so the system will correctly say 2. Demonstrate 10–14 September instead, or the right answer will look like a bug.

Found on the way

Queueing the notifications surfaced a defect outside this module. A listener in AppServiceProvider cleared the active tenant whenever a queued job finished. On a worker that is harmless; on the sync connection the job runs inside a request, so everything after it wrote rows with a null company — which row level security then makes invisible. It now restores whatever tenant was active before the job.

The one thing still outside a test suite

§47’s hundred companies of a thousand employees needs hardware, not another assertion. leave-bureau:benchmark seeds it, times seeding, processing, export, reconciliation and reporting, prints milliseconds per employee and employees per second, and deletes what it made. It refuses to run in production, and refuses to seed at all if the module is off.
