# Board & Committee Management
### The complete, AI-powered governance platform — King IV aligned

**Run a flawless board. From the first notice to the signed minute — secure, paperless, and intelligent.**

Mkhaya Hub Board & Committee Management gives your directors, company secretary and committees one secure home for everything that makes governance work: meetings, packs, resolutions, minutes, compliance and risk — now supercharged with AI that prepares, scans and summarises so your people focus on decisions, not paperwork.

---

## Why boards choose Mkhaya Hub

- ✅ **End-to-end** — the entire meeting lifecycle in one place: schedule → notice → pack → meet → vote → minute → archive.
- 🔒 **Bank-grade security** — Azure AD sign-on, two-factor authentication, encrypted votes, watermarked packs, full audit trails.
- 🇿🇦 **King IV by design** — a built-in compliance tracker mapped to all 16 principles with evidence.
- 🤖 **AI that does the heavy lifting** — risk scanning, draft minutes, board-pack pre-reads, agenda drafting and a governance assistant that answers questions across your records.
- 📱 **Anywhere access** — read packs and annotate offline; sync the moment you reconnect.

## What's inside

### 1. Director & Board Register
Maintain a single source of truth for your board.
- Executive, non-executive, independent and alternate director classification
- Independence status, appointment, resignation and disqualification tracking
- **Automatic term & rotation alerts** — six-month advance warnings before a term lapses, with full history and audit trail
- Support for external directors without system accounts

### 2. Committees
Structure and govern every committee — Audit, Risk, Finance, Procurement, Ethics, Social & Ethics, and ad-hoc.
- Mandates, objectives and terms of reference
- Configurable **quorum** (number or percentage) and voting thresholds
- Chair and secretary assignment; member roles (chair, secretary, member, observer)

### 3. Meetings — the full lifecycle
From scheduling to archival, with nothing falling through the cracks.
- Board, committee, AGM, extraordinary, special and **round-robin** meeting types
- Recurring meetings, virtual links, hybrid support
- **Quorum tracking**, adjournment & reconvening, formal status workflow
- Notices (initial, reminder, final), RSVP and live attendance / apologies
- Calendar export

### 4. Agenda Builder
Professional agendas in minutes.
- Drag-and-drop ordering, hierarchical sub-items, standing items
- Item types: decision, discussion, information, procedural
- Presenter and time allocation per item
- **Reusable templates** per committee type, with full version history
- 🤖 **AI agenda drafting** — generate a complete, well-ordered agenda from standing items, unfinished actions and open resolutions

### 5. Board Packs
Compile, secure and distribute professional board packs.
- One-click compile to a single watermarked PDF (cover, contents, agenda, documents, reports, resolutions)
- Classification levels: public, internal, confidential, restricted
- Secure distribution with **read receipts** and download tracking
- Versioning and revisions
- 🤖 **AI pre-read brief** — every pack distilled into a one-page "what you need to decide" summary per director

### 6. Offline Access & Annotations
Directors prepare anywhere — on a plane, off the grid.
- Download packs for offline reading
- Private notes, highlights and questions
- **Two-way sync** when back online

### 7. Resolutions & Voting
Capture, propose, vote and track every resolution.
- Ordinary, special, written and round-robin resolutions with auto-numbering (RES-YYYY-###)
- **Secure voting**: show-of-hands, secret ballot, encrypted vote casts
- Configurable thresholds (simple majority, two-thirds, three-quarters, unanimous)
- Chair casting vote and **conflict-of-interest exclusion** with admin override
- Proposer / seconder, voting results and pass/fail outcomes

### 8. Resolution & Action Register
Make sure decisions actually get done.
- Implementation tracking with owners, due dates and % progress
- Priority levels, escalation, automated reminders
- **Evidence upload** and completion approval
- Cross-resolution action register for a single view of all outstanding actions
- 🤖 **AI action insight** — flags at-risk actions and drafts reminder / escalation messages

### 9. Meeting Minutes
Draft, approve, sign and distribute — defensibly.
- Structured minute items linked to agenda items
- Discussion summaries, decisions and voting outcomes
- Formal workflow: draft → submitted → chair-approved → **signed** → distributed
- Action items extracted and assigned
- Version history and amendments
- 🤖 **AI minutes generation** from a meeting transcript, plus an **AI quality gate** that checks completeness against King IV before approval

### 10. Conflicts of Interest
- Annual, ad-hoc and meeting-specific declarations
- Conflict categories, required recusal and approval workflow
- Linked directly to voting exclusions

### 11. Gift & Hospitality Register
- Gifts received and given, with counterparty and value
- Categories: gift, hospitality, travel, entertainment, donation, sponsorship
- Review and approval workflow (approved, declined, returned, retained)

### 12. King IV Compliance Tracker
Demonstrate good governance with evidence.
- All **16 King IV principles** with status tracking
- Responsible owner, review dates and application notes
- Evidence upload per principle
- Compliance dashboard at a glance

### 13. Governance Document Library
- Central repository for policies, charters, contracts and reports
- Version control and classification
- Download / print controls, watermarking
- **Full access audit log** (who viewed and downloaded what)
- Full-text search across documents

### 14. Board Roles & Delegations
- Assign Administrator, Company Secretary, Chairperson, Director, Committee Member, Internal Auditor and Observer roles
- Temporary delegations (e.g. acting chairperson)
- Bulk role matrix management

### 15. Organisation & Legal Entity Register
- Corporate structure: holding, subsidiaries, business units, regional offices, joint ventures
- **Legal-entity register** with registration numbers, jurisdictions and ownership
- **Statutory filings** tracking with due dates and overdue alerts

### 16. Board Skills Matrix *(new)*
Build the right board — and prove it.
- Define target competencies (digital, financial, cyber, ESG, legal…) with coverage targets
- Rate each director's proficiency and visualise the matrix
- 🤖 **AI gap analysis** — highlights under-covered skills, diversity and tenure gaps, and recommends recruitment priorities

### 17. Questionnaires & Board Evaluations *(new)*
- D&O disclosures, conflict attestations, board and committee self-evaluations
- Build custom questionnaires (text, scale, choice, boolean)
- Assign to directors, track completion, collect responses

### 18. AI Register — Oversight of AI *(new)*
Govern the AI your organisation uses.
- Catalogue every AI system, its purpose, data sensitivity and autonomy
- 🤖 **AI-assisted risk rating** and the controls the board should expect
- Built for the emerging board duty to oversee AI

### 19. Regulatory & Market Monitor *(new)*
- 🤖 AI-curated regulatory, sector and peer briefings
- "Why the board should care" summaries, tagged to governance areas

## The AI Governance Suite 🤖

Built in, not bolted on. Every AI output is a **draft for human review** — never auto-final — and every call is **audit-logged** for defensibility.

| **Smart Risk Scanner** | Flags litigation, compliance, financial and disclosure red-flags inside packs, minutes and resolutions |
| **Governance Assistant** | Ask plain-English questions across your governance records and get cited answers (semantic search) |

| **AI Minutes** | Draft minutes and extract action items from a meeting transcript |
| **Minutes QA Gate** | Checks minutes for completeness against King IV before approval |
| **Agenda Drafting** | Generates a structured agenda from carried-forward business |
| **Meeting Effectiveness** | Participation balance, decision velocity and unresolved-item analytics |
| **Skills-Gap Analysis** | Board composition vs target matrix, with recommendations |
| **AI Risk Rating** | Risk-rates entries in your AI register |
