# Mkhaya Hub CRM — Complete Capabilities

**The AI-native CRM that runs your whole revenue engine.**
From first touch to closed-won to lifelong customer — leads, deals, conversations, campaigns, service and analytics in one platform, with an AI co-pilot woven through every step. Built for South African business: multilingual, WhatsApp-first, POPIA-aware.

> Every capability in this brochure is live in the product — not a roadmap.

---

## 1. Sales Pipeline & Deal Management

**See every opportunity, move it forward, and never let one slip.**

- **Unlimited pipelines & stages** — run separate motions (Enterprise, SMB, Renewals) with per-stage win probability, SLA day-limits and colour coding
- **Visual kanban** — drag-and-drop deals between stages; probability and close status update automatically
- **Rich deal records** — amount, currency, expected close, account & contact, owner, days-in-stage, full notes & activity timeline
- **AI win-probability** — predicts 0–100% likelihood to close with a confidence level and reasoning
- **AI deal-risk detection** — flags stalling, slipping close dates and long stage tenure as low/medium/high risk
- **AI lost-deal analysis** — categorises why deals are lost, judges how avoidable it was, coaches the rep, and flags recurring loss patterns (e.g. "you keep losing on price to Competitor X")
- **AI deal coach** — next-best-action, full deal strategy (situation, steps, risks) and AI-drafted follow-up emails/WhatsApp per channel
- **Manager alerts** — automatic notification to managers and sales leaders when a deal is lost

## 2. Leads — Capture, Score, Convert

**Turn raw interest into qualified pipeline automatically.**

- **AI lead scoring** — 0–100 fit score with priority and reasoning, used to sort and focus effort
- **One-click conversion** — transactionally creates the account, contact and an opening deal in your default pipeline
- **Inbound lead capture from email** — monitored mailboxes auto-create leads from incoming mail
- **AI email triage** — classifies inbound mail (lead / enquiry / support / spam…), scores spam confidence, and **auto-extracts** name, company, email, phone and job title
- **Auto-engagement** — new leads get a templated welcome email and WhatsApp confirmation, and the assigned rep is notified instantly
- **Status workflow** — New → Contacted → Qualified → Converted, with de-duplication on capture

## 3. Contacts & Accounts (360° + Health)

**Know every customer relationship at a glance.**

- **Account 360°** — contacts, open/won/lost deals, revenue by period, and a unified activity timeline across the account, its contacts and its deals
- **Account hierarchy** — link subsidiaries to parent companies and roll up metrics
- **AI account-health scoring** — fuses NPS/CSAT, complaint volume & severity, activity recency and deal outcomes into a 0–100 health score, churn-risk rating and **upsell/expansion flags**, with recommended plays
- **Health dashboard** — every account ranked by health, worst-first, with on-demand AI re-assessment
- **AI data enrichment** — infer industry, size band and description from a company name/website to fill the blanks

## 4. Activities, Tasks & "My Day"

**Everything you touch, logged — and an AI plan for your day.**

- **Unified timeline** — calls, emails, meetings, tasks, reminders, notes, WhatsApp and SMS against any record
- **Tasks & reminders** — personal or record-linked, with due dates, overdue flags and one-click complete; log an activity and spawn a follow-up in the same step
- **AI conversation summary** — paste a transcript or thread; get a summary, sentiment and extracted action items
- **"My Day" AI planner** — weighs **everything** about the rep (open tasks, reminders, slipping deals, hot leads, recent notes and activities) and returns the prioritised top-10 actions for today, each with why-it-matters, a concrete action and a priority

## 5. Conversation Intelligence (Calls & Meetings)

**Gong-style insight on every conversation.**

- **Click-to-call** — integrated dialing via SIP softphone, Twilio, Vonage and Telnyx, with automatic call logging
- **Meeting recording & transcription** — upload audio (MP3/MP4/WAV/WebM/OGG); async speech-to-text via Whisper-compatible AI
- **AI call/meeting scoring** — quality score (0–100), customer sentiment, rep talk-ratio, objections raised, commitments made, agreed next steps and 2–3 coaching tips
- **Auto follow-ups** — schedule a dated task/reminder straight off a meeting

## 6. Forecasting, Targets & Performance

**A number you can trust, and the plays to hit it.**

- **Targets** — set weekly & monthly quotas across 7 metrics (calls, emails, meetings, activities, new leads, deals won, revenue)
- **Forecast dashboard** — open value, weighted forecast, won MTD, commit, and per-rep gap-to-quota with attainment %
- **AI forecast insights** — a headline call, commit confidence, the biggest risks, and specific recommended plays (who should do what)
- **AI pipeline anomaly detection** — surfaces sandbagging, stuck deals, stale deals and data errors
- **Dashboards for every level** — rep ("My Dashboard" weekly/monthly scorecards + trend charts), team (per-report metrics, attainment, totals) and executive (org win rate, pipeline value, lead-conversion, top-10 leaderboard)

## 7. Campaigns (Email & WhatsApp)

**Reach the right audience and measure every open and click.**

- **Audience building** — by industry, account or owner; or bulk CSV upload with de-duplication
- **Lifecycle & scheduling** — draft → schedule → send, with a configurable daily send cap for deliverability, plus test sends
- **Open & click tracking** — pixel-based opens and wrapped-link clicks, per-recipient and campaign-level
- **Engagement segmentation** — filter recipients by opened/clicked/bounced/unsubscribed
- **Deliverability & compliance** — provider event webhooks (Mailgun/SendGrid/Postmark) for bounces/complaints, one-click unsubscribe and automatic suppression lists

## 8. Sequences / Cadences

**Automated, multi-step outreach that stops when a human replies.**

- **Multi-step builder** — mix email and WhatsApp steps with day-offsets and merge tags
- **AI sequence drafting** — describe the goal; AI generates a 2–8 step cadence
- **Smart enrollment** — from leads or accounts (by industry/spend/list), with suppression checks
- **AI reply classification** — detects interested / not-interested / out-of-office / unsubscribe and can **auto-stop** the cadence and suggest a reply
- **Live stats** — active, replied, completed and interested counts per sequence

## 9. Omnichannel Messaging

**Meet customers where they are.**

- **Email** — reusable merge-tag templates (lead/deal/complaint/campaign/general), system auto-responders, and provider integrations (Gmail, Microsoft 365, SendGrid, Mailgun, SES, Postmark, SMTP)
- **WhatsApp Business** — Meta Cloud API, free-form and approved-template messages; used in campaigns and sequences
- **SMS** — provider integrations for text outreach
- **Inbound mailboxes** — IMAP/POP3 monitoring with AI filtering that routes mail to leads or complaints automatically

## 10. Customer Service — Complaints & Compliments

**Capture, triage and resolve feedback with SLAs.**

- **Public portal** — branded, no-login complaint/compliment form; also via monitored mailbox or manual entry
- **AI triage** — classifies type, severity (low→critical), category and sentiment; identifies the staff member involved; writes a summary and recommends corrective actions/coaching
- **SLA engine** — respond-by, resolve-by and escalate-after timers with automatic overdue flagging and severity-driven escalation to managers
- **Full case lifecycle** — new → acknowledged → in-progress → escalated → resolved → closed, with a complete update timeline, auto customer acknowledgement and reference numbers (e.g. C-2026-00001)

## 11. Surveys (NPS / CSAT / Feedback)

**Measure loyalty and act on it.**

- **Templated surveys** — NPS, CSAT, product and support templates, or build your own question set
- **Targeted audiences** — all contacts, by industry, by spend, or specific accounts, with live audience preview
- **Token-based collection** — unique per-recipient links, public response form, NPS/CSAT extraction
- **Recurring planner** — one-off or weekly/monthly/quarterly cadences with deadlines, auto-advance, pause/resume
- **AI survey analysis** — executive summary, sentiment, recurring themes, prioritised recommendations and quick wins

## 12. AI Copilot

**Ask your CRM anything; let it draft and act.**

- **Natural-language Q&A** over your own data ("show at-risk deals with no activity in 14 days", "which deals close this month?")
- **Respects visibility** — only reasons over records the user is allowed to see
- **Proposes safe actions** for confirmation — move a deal's stage, create a task, draft an email or WhatsApp — drafts are never sent without you

## 13. Automation & Workflows (No-Code)

**Codify your sales process once; let it run forever.**

- **Triggers** — lead.created, deal.created, deal.stage_changed, deal.inactive, deal.sla_breached, activity.completed
- **Conditions** — field rules with full operator set and AND/OR logic; fire only when a deal enters a specific stage
- **Actions** — create task, add note, update field, reassign owner, escalate (reassign + priority task + approver alert)
- **Full run log** — every execution recorded (success/skipped/failed) with run counts for compliance and tuning

## 14. Data Management — Import, Hygiene, Dedup

**Get clean data in, keep it clean, and undo mistakes.**

- **CSV import** — for leads, contacts, accounts and deals; auto-mapping suggestions, delimiter auto-detection, Excel/Mac/Windows encoding handling and downloadable templates
- **Duplicate strategies** — skip / update / create on import, with per-entity dedup keys
- **Mandatory-field rules** — configurable required fields per entity, per company; an import is **blocked** if a required field is unmapped or any row is missing it
- **One-click reverse** — every imported record is stamped to its import, so a corrupted upload can be rolled back (soft-delete, recoverable), including auto-created accounts
- **Duplicate detection & merge** — cluster look-alikes, then merge: fill blanks, re-parent activities, reassign deals/contacts, soft-delete the loser — all transactional
- **Data-hygiene dashboard** — incomplete records, stale deals (60d+) and stale leads (30d+), plus AI account enrichment to fill gaps

## 15. Extensibility — Custom Fields & Objects

**Model your business without a developer.**

- **Custom fields** — text, number, date, boolean, select, multiselect on leads/contacts/accounts/deals; required flags, ordering, activate/hide — zero schema migrations
- **Custom objects** — build entirely new entities (Projects, Contracts, Assets…) with their own fields, icon and label, and manage their records in the CRM

## 16. Reports, Analytics & KPIs

**Out-of-the-box insight plus build-your-own metrics.**

- **Standard reports** — summary KPIs, sales funnel with stage conversion, weighted forecast by month, source attribution, win/loss analysis, and 12-month trends (deals created, won, leads, calls/emails)
- **Custom KPI builder** — count/sum/avg/min/max over any entity with filters and currency/percent/number formatting
- **AI report narration** — turns the numbers into a written executive summary
- **Scheduled reports** — daily/weekly/monthly delivery to any recipients, run via background jobs
- **CSV export** — every report exports for Power BI / Tableau
- **Saved views & segments** — reusable, shareable filters per entity

## 17. Integrations, API & Webhooks

**Connect the CRM to everything else.**

- **Integrations hub** — email (Gmail, M365, SendGrid, Mailgun, SES, Postmark, SMTP), telephony (Twilio, Vonage), WhatsApp, SMS, and video meetings (Zoom, Teams, Google Meet) — with connection testing and encrypted, masked credentials
- **Outbound webhooks** — subscribe to lead/deal/activity events; HMAC-SHA256 signed payloads, delivery logging, secret rotation
- **REST API** — token-based access (Laravel Sanctum); tokens inherit the user's roles & visibility, revocable any time

## 18. Collaboration & Governance

**Work as a team, with controls leadership trusts.**

- **Discussions & @mentions** — threaded comments on any record with mention notifications
- **Approvals** — discount/deal/custom approval requests routed to an approver, with decisions, notes and a "pending vs mine" dashboard
- **Role-based visibility** — reps see their own, managers see their team subtree, executives/admins see all — enforced on every list, report and the Copilot
- **Audit trail** — every change captured (who, what, when, old → new), filterable by entity/action/date and exportable to CSV
- **Enterprise foundations** — soft-deletes, transactional safety, encrypted secrets and graceful AI degradation (everything works even with AI switched off)
